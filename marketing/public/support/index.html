Support · contact and reporting

Reach a human. Get a straight answer.

This page is the public support and reporting channel for Riot. It names the responsible operator, states what response to expect on this channel, and is explicit about which in-app controls are not shipped yet.

Contact the operator

One named operator. One public channel.

Riot is a protest.net project. The responsible operator for support, reports, and escalation is reachable directly; there is no ticket queue and no outsourced triage.

Support and general questions

Email rabble@protest.net for help with the app, the site, or your community's data.

Include your platform (iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android phone, or Android tablet) and what you were trying to do.

Reports and escalation

Report objectionable content, abuse, or safety concerns to rabble@protest.net with the subject prefix REPORT:.

If you believe someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first, then email with the subject prefix URGENT:.

Response commitments — public channel

What response to expect, and when.

These commitments apply to reports sent to the public email channel above. They are the same targets recorded in Riot's release policy source, and they bind the operator, not the software.

Acknowledgement
  • Every report to the public channel is acknowledged within 24 hours.
  • Reports about imminent harm or illegal content receive a decision within 24 hours.
  • Other objectionable-content reports receive a decision within 72 hours.
What a decision means
  • A human reviews the report; nothing is auto-actioned.
  • You receive the outcome and the reason on the same channel.
  • If the report needs information only you have, the clock pauses while we ask, and we say so.

Early-access honesty

In-app controls that are not shipped yet.

Version 1.0 is an early-access release. The public channel above works today; the in-app controls below are designed and recorded as release gates, but they are not in the build you can install. This page will change when they ship.

Not in the app yet
  • In-app content reporting and author reporting flows.
  • In-app objectionable-content filtering and local author blocking.
  • Published prohibited-content rules tied to the app.
  • Moderator response workflows linked to protocol tombstones.
What to do until they ship
  • Use the email channel above for anything you would report in-app.
  • Leave a community whose content you do not want; your data stays on your devices.
  • Read the privacy page for the current data posture before joining public spaces.

Version 1.0 is an early-access release. Riot is free, with no in-app purchases. See the releases page for what is in this build and the accessibility page for our rehearsal commitments.
